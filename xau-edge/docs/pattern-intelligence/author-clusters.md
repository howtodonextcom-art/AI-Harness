# Author clusters

## One author, 29 Reels

All 29 Reels supplied by the owner come from **one Facebook account, "Zenix Arixon"** (the same account as the earlier Wolfe Wave reel). Reposts and re-edits by one author are one voice: 29 items are **one source cluster**, not 29 independent confirmations. Identity beyond the account name is unknown; no profile, website or other platform account was found (the profile requires a login and was not bypassed).

## Is there a stable, repeatable method?

Two different questions, with different answers.

**Presentation: yes, highly stable.** Every Reel uses the same template: a textbook diagram of the pattern in the upper panel, a replayed chart in the lower panel that is annotated step by step (trendlines, pattern lines, a circled entry marker with an "ENTRY LONG/SHORT" label, a "STOP-LOSS" label, an arrow to a target), music instead of speech, a caption that names the pattern with a promotional hook ("90% traders miss this", "powerful reversal signal").

**Trading method: not demonstrated.** What a trader would need is absent in all 29: no instrument, no timeframe, no price scale, no numeric rule for the geometry, no stop distance rule, no position sizing, no statement of how targets are managed, no count of examples taken, no failures. The entry is drawn at a different structural event for different families (below), and every example ends favourably: **27 of 27 single-pattern examples**. That is the signature of selected, hindsight-annotated examples, not of a rule set. Verdict: **posts selected chart examples**; a repeatable method cannot be established from this cluster.

## Production variants inside the cluster

* Studio-background template (most Reels). Reels 23, 25, 27 and 28 use a different, zoomed and meme-edited style with film or TV clips (23 also with an "EASY TRADING" watermark); 26 shares the zoomed style without clips. That is either a change of editor or content taken from another channel; it was **not** established which. Third-party clips are copyrighted content of others.
* Near-duplicate: 19 and 22 (the same bearish Adam and Eve chart recording, different captions).
* Reel 17 shows a drawing-tool colour palette on screen and Reel 26 begins with drawing close-ups: the annotations are drawn over an existing chart in front of the viewer. This is evidence of the way the clips are made, not of when the pattern was identified relative to the price move.

## Claims in captions (CLAIMED_BY_AUTHOR)

Examples: "90% Traders Miss This Powerful Reversal", "Powerful Bullish Reversal Pattern", "Big Crash Coming?", "Next Breakout About to Explode?". The "90%" is about traders who miss the pattern, it is not a win rate, and it has no source. No caption states a win rate, sample or profit. Views and reactions in the titles (up to 152K views) were recorded and not used.

## Cross-source agreement

External sources agree with the *geometry* the author draws for the double top and bottom, wedges and Wolfe Wave, and disagree with the author's confidence: Bulkowski ranks the rising wedge down breakout (Reel 13) last of 36 (51% break-even failure) and calls the falling wedge (Reels 2, 10, 14) a poor performer; his Adam-and-Eve statistics are idealised and he found that buying a double bottom before it confirms fails 48% of the time.
